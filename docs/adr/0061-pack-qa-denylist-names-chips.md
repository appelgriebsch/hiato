# ADR 0061: Shared pack QA (#366) — slur denylist, name-list gate, chip gate, EU Portuguese

- **Status**: Proposed
- **Date**: 2026-11-24
- **Deciders**: Andreas Gerlach
- **Supersedes**: none (sharpens ADR 0023, ADR 0028, ADR 0040, ADR 0058, ADR 0060)
- **Related issues**: #366. Does not close #335, #357, #358, #359, #360, or #361.

## Context

Ask Avery's recheck at `8c94bdc` still found slurs and vulgar insults in grown slots, person-name lemmas whose glosses were invented common nouns (so the old "name list AND person-name gloss" rule let them ship), synonym chips that repeat the lemma under a different spelling (`LAß`/`LASS`, `GRÜSSE`/`Grüße`) or that are not words (`3º`, `CO2`), and a set of European-Portuguese findings (places, pre-AO1990 spelling, accentless misspellings, a few wrong glosses and chips). Slots 0–399 cannot move (ADR 0058). Production stays at `8c94bdc`; this change does not deploy.

## Decision

1. **Slur and vulgar-insult denylist.** Ten language-scoped lines were added to `lemma-denylist.txt` (`en GOOK`, `pt BOCHES`, `fr POUFFIASSE`, `it INVERTITO`, `it BATTONA`, `de FLITTCHEN`, `de FETTSACK`, `es FOLLADOR`, `es LAMECULOS`, `de DRECKSACK`). Matching is `spellingKey`, so case and German `ß`/`SS` fold. `validatePack` already rejects a denylisted lemma; the new lines plus a regression test are the CI guard. Each hit is replaced in its own slot. The curated profanity list LDNOOBW is not imported. It is used once as a scan aid. Hits that are slurs or vulgar insults and still ship are added as their own language-scoped lines (`es SUBNORMAL`, `es FURCIA`, `fr GROGNASSE`, `pt RAMEIRA`, `fr PISSER`, `fr BANDER`, `it CHIAPPA`, `it POMICIARE`, `it PIRLA`, `it DITALINO`). The `DITO` family drops `ditalino`, and the `URINER` chip `pisser` is removed, so the gate cache matches the pack.

2. **Name list, every language.** `okLemma` / gloss rejection / FR-IT `accept` no longer skip `fr` and `it`. They call `listedNameRejected`. A lemma on `lemma-names.txt` fails `packs:check` with `person-name list` whatever the gloss says. Homographs that are real common words stay only with a reasoned line in `lemma-names-allow.txt` (`lang WORD # reason`). The allowlist holds (a) every name-list lemma already in slots 0–399, because those slots cannot change, and (b) a grown name-list lemma only when the reason states the common-word reading. The shared boilerplate "not a proper-name headword" is not a reason. `isPersonNameGloss` also matches `nome maschile` / `nome femminile` / `abbreviazione di un nome` and `nom d'origine` / `nom italien`. German is never treated as proper-name-by-capital, because `dictionary-de` capitalizes nouns. New lemmas are never name-listed. Places, people, brands, and abbreviations called out for PT are appended to `lemma-names.txt`, except `BRA`, `PENCE`, and `LAS`, which are ordinary words in other pack languages and are replaced in the PT pack only. Adding `GPS` and `DVD` also replaces the grown ES slots of those abbreviations. `de/a1` MARK stays: it is slot 175.

3. **Chips.** `CHIP_LANG_GATE_LANGS` is every pack language that has a Hunspell dictionary. A chip fails when `spellingKey` equals the lemma, or when it contains a Unicode number (`CO2`, `O₂`) or no letter. `3º` fails because of the digit; U+00BA is a letter. A hyphenated or apostrophe form is tested whole before it is split. English membership is en-US or en-GB (`dictionary-en` and `dictionary-en-gb`). Real words those dictionaries miss are on the loanword allowlist. The `GRÜSSE` and `LAß` lemmas are not retagged or rebanded (#335, #357); only the self-chip is removed. Chips in slots 0–399 that the corrected gate accepts are put back. Chips that are not words of the pack language stay off. Counts for the slots 0–399 chip edits (#369 S1): 162 entries touched, slot stability counted by word (zero lemma words changed in slots 0–399), and 593 chips removed in total — the earlier 572 figure undercounted.

Pejorative labels for people are not valid new lemmas (`WEICHLING`, `CINGLÉ`, `MORVEUX`, `DENTUÇA`, `CAIPIRA` are replaced). `ZICKE`, `PENNER`, and `IMBÉCILE` already shipped before this ticket and stay until their own language tickets. `CRETINO` also already shipped, in `pt/b1` slot 480, but there is no PT ticket for it, so that slot is now `GRAVATA`.

4. **European Portuguese, AO1990.** The app is pt-PT. Citation forms come from `dictionary-pt-pt` (a headword, not an inflected `[$infinitive$…]` line). Accentless misspellings `NOTICIAS`, `CLINICA`, and `ULTIMA` are replaced and dropped from the accent-pair allowlist. The nine `-MOS` pairs (`FALAMOS`/`FALÁMOS`, and the same pattern for acabar, chegar, gostar, levar, passar, pensar, procurar, voltar) stay: they are EU present versus pretérito, not spelling duplicates, and several unaccented forms sit in slots 0–399. `COLECÇÃO` is replaced by a different in-band word because `COLEÇÃO` already occupies `pt/c1` slot 288. Off-band inflected or clitic rows named in #366 (`SABONETE`, `CALMAS`, `CELEBRA`, `OBSERVOU`, `MORDA`, `LHO`, `LHOS`, and the place/brand rows) are replaced in their own slots. `CHEF` and `ASCENDER` are re-glossed. The wrong chips on `MATEM`, `COSTELAS`, `ASAS`, `SOUBER`, `TERCEIRO`, and `CARBONO` are fixed or removed. `ESTARÁS` / `FIZEMOS` / `TIVER` stay.

5. **German info rows.** `BERUF` and `BIBLIOTHEK` are replaced in their slots with in-band words. `NOCHMAL` keeps its adverb POS; the gloss is `erneut.`

Replacements are same-slot (ADR 0058). Pack lengths are unchanged. Every changed pack bumps `version` and `pack-pin.json` is regenerated. New PT lemmas are `pos-exempt` (no licensed PT POS source). Other new lemmas take a content POS from wordhoard or CEFR-J, corrected when that source tag is not the real content POS and the right tag is one of noun, verb, adjective, or adverb.

## Options Considered

| Option | Pros | Cons |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A — allowlist for base slots and common-stem homographs (chosen) | Slots 0–399 stay; real words such as `ROSA` and `VENTO` keep shipping; a listed name with an invented gloss still fails | A literal "no name-listed lemma ships" is true only after the allowlist exemption |
| B — delete every name-list hit | Matches the sentence of the acceptance criterion literally | Rewrites slots 0–399 and deletes common nouns; forbidden by ADR 0058 |
| C — seed LDNOOBW into the denylist | Wider profanity net | Not small or reviewable; out of scope for the ten listed items |
| D — drop one member of each `-MOS` pair | One spelling per pair | They are different tenses, and some sit in slots 0–399 |

## Amendment: #369 follow-ups (2026-11-25)

- **R1 allowlist rule.** Every `lemma-names-allow.txt` line for a slot ≥ 400 must carry a person-written reason, not a copy of the slot's gloss; `scripts/lemma-names.test.ts` fails when the reason contains the referenced slot's gloss. The per-language fr/it rewrites are tracked in #360 and #361 and are exempt from the test until they land.
- **S4 PT exclusion.** `PT_EXPAND_EXCLUDE` (`BRA`, `PENCE`, `LAS`) in `scripts/pack-select.ts` keeps those pt proper nouns out of every `packs:expand` path; they stay ordinary words in en/fr and off the global name list.
- **W8 call-site tests.** `scripts/expand-packs-name-gate.test.ts` fails when any of the three generation-side `listedNameRejected` call sites in `scripts/expand-packs.ts` (`okLemma`, `glossRejected`, the FR/IT `accept`) is reverted.
- **PT pack QA.** The full PT review (all slots of all six pt packs, per the ADR 0058 amendment) replaced the invented-gloss name lemmas `TIM` (pt/b1/101), `ABRAM` (pt/b1/202), `COLE` (pt/b1/265) in their own slots, and the above-band nouns `GRAVATA` (pt/b1/480), `ANANÁS` (pt/c1/778), `VINAGRE` (pt/c1/959) (#369 N1). Gloss style: `TENISTA` names ténis, and the nine EU-PT `-MOS` pairs state presente vs pretérito in the gloss, with EU clitic order in `FALAMOS`.

## Amendment: #359 ES pack QA (2026-11-26)

- **Chips.** The wrong-language/English chips listed in #359 were already replaced by #367. A full re-scan of all six es packs (every slot 0–999) through the chip gate (`invalidSynonymChipReason` with `isWordOfLang('es', …)`) finds zero invalid chips, so every shipped es chip is a valid Spanish word or an allowlisted loanword.
- **"retrasado".** The word no longer appears in any es pack field. Only thing/action senses were present; es/b1 `ATRASADO` now chips `tardío`/`lento`, es/c2 `DEMORADO` is re-glossed "que tarda más de lo previsto." with chips `atrasado`/`tardío`, and es/c2 `DIFERIDO` reads "pospuesto o atrasado en el tiempo." No person-referring use remains.
- **N1.** es/c1 slot 596 `BLUSA` (above-band easy noun) is replaced in its own slot by `PERJUICIO` (wordhoard c1 band, rank 14319; pos `noun` from the wordhoard pos map; gated example from `example-sentences`, ipa/family from `lemma-extras`). Hint-ceiling fallout in lower packs fixed: a1 `DAÑO` chip `perjuicio`→`mal`, a1 `PELIGROSO` and a2 `DAÑAR` glosses reworded off the c1 token and the `DAÑAR` stem.
- **S8.** es/c2 slot 121 `CHACHA` stays (slot pinned, ADR 0058) and is re-glossed neutrally: "Persona que realiza las tareas domésticas en una casa.", chips `criada`/`empleada`. Policy decision: the domestic-work sense is the word's standard dictionary meaning; no derogatory reading is glossed.
- **Proper-noun lemmas.** The full review (all slots 0–999 of all six es packs) found two place lemmas with invented common-noun glosses: es/c2/821 `MILÁN` → `INCENTIVO` and es/c2/983 `SERBIA` → `PERICIA`, both replaced in their own slots (wordhoard c2 band, pos from the wordhoard pos map). No name-list lemma ships (`listedNameRejected`), no other place/brand lemma found, no denylist hit (`packs:check`), no invalid chip.
- **Extras.** `lemma-extras --lang=es` filled ipa/family where the sources provide them (wiktextract primary, gated Mistral fallback; ipa 89%, family 94% of 5,854 es lemmas) and the residual gap is reported; `example-sentences --lang=es` gated the new examples. Markdown asterisks stripped from the `MÉTEMELA` and `MESANA` examples (gate log sentences updated to match). Artifacts: `lemma-extras-gate.json`, `example-gate.json`, pack versions, `pack-pin.json`.

## Amendment: #360 FR pack QA (2026-11-27)

- **Full review.** Every lemma of all six `fr` packs was reviewed across all slots 0–999 (ADR 0058 amendment, #370), not a sample: the name list + allowlist, the denylist, Hunspell membership, the person/place gloss gates, chip language membership, POS-vs-gloss, markdown in examples, and English-word/brand/place lemmas.
- **77 same-slot replacements (ADR 0058).** Proper nouns (person names `ZOÉ TRUMP TOTO EPSTEIN STEINER MOREAU MOBY BECKETT DARWIN OSCAR HODGE UTHER BARRA NEUMANN BERLUSCONI DUCHAMP VERMEER PUCCINI KLIMT`, brands `VOLVO HYUNDAI JEEP`, places `TITANIC YOKOHAMA`, titles/dynasties `BOURBON ESQUIRE`), English words (`STREET HOUSE COMICS WRITER BILL MILE BOY LORD BOBBY SCOTT ANDY ROB CAP ALTER MANU JASPER ROBIN`), inflected verb forms and participles (`ALLAI VERREZ SAURAI TIENNE SERA APPELLE JETTE DONNE SENT TUE ARMÉ RÊVÉ FRAPPÉ ÉLEVÉ MÉRITÉ REPÉRÉ RELÂCHÉ`), abbreviations (`MLLE`), person-derived units (`HENRY FARADAY KELVIN`), vulgar words (`MERDER PET PIPI`), explicit sexual items (`ORGASME FELLATION TAPINEUSE TAPINEUR SEXTO`), and the N1 above-band nouns (`CITRON PULL KETCHUP MANGUE TOURISTE`). Each was replaced in its own slot by an in-band wordhoard pick with a hand-reviewed French gloss, wordhoard POS (recorded in `pos-overrides.txt`), gate-clean chips, a gated example, and wiktextract/Mistral extras.
- **VIOL stays.** `fr/a2` `VIOL` keeps its clinical legal gloss ("agression sexuelle grave") and ships no example; reviewed for A2 — the word is a standard crime noun and the gloss is not graphic, so it is not borderline content under the pack policy.
- **POS fixes.** `LEVER`→verb, `JACK`→noun, `PECTORAL`→adjective, `AMPUTÉ`/`JÉSUITE`→noun, plus 25 more name/loanword entries whose wordhoard priority pick contradicted the shipped gloss (`pos-overrides.txt`, #357 mechanism).
- **Chips and examples.** `PRÊTER` loses the antonym chip `emprunter`; `PLAINTIF` chips `demandeur/requérant` replaced by `larmoyant/triste`; `CÂPRE` and `BIZUTAGE` lose wrong chips; `COUVERT` is re-glossed as the adjective ("qui est à l'abri") with a matching example; markdown `**…**` stripped from the `MATT`, `ALEC`, `AMIR`, `BAN`, `LÉVITIQUE` examples and single `*…*` stripped from the `DING` (b2), `GAP`, `CARTOON`, `SPLASH`, `MILLMAN` (c2) examples (gate-log sentences updated to match); the ungrammatical `LESBIEN` example fixed; the nonsense `DIGITAL` example replaced.
- **S6 gloss style.** `CARACTÈRE`, `PROMOTION`, `TROUBLE`, `PHARE` re-glossed to a single sense; `POIVRIER` names its spice without the stem (`arbrisseau dont les baies servent d'épice`); `DESTINÉE` loses the English "fate"; `POIREAUTER` re-glossed "faire attendre quelqu'un" (it does not mean to make a shrill sound). `VINGTAINE` keeps its gloss: the stem-spoiler gate (ADR 0023) forbids "vingt" in the gloss of `VINGTAINE`, so the number cannot be named.
- **Name-gloss gate widened (#24 slip).** `isFrPersonGloss` now also fails `prénommé(e)` (the HILDE shape "une femme prénommé d'origine germanique") and the bare name-class words `nom de famille`, `nom propre`, `surnom`, `nom féminin/masculin` for any lemma, even off the name list; regression tests in `name-gloss.test.ts`.
- **R1 allowlist.** All 210 `fr` lines for slots ≥ 400 now carry a person-written reason (common-word reading + slot), 29 lines for replaced words were dropped, and `lemma-names.test.ts` no longer delegates `fr` to this issue. Seven `fr` accent-pair allowlist entries whose words were replaced were removed.
- **Artifacts.** Six `fr` packs bumped `version`; `pack-pin.json` refreshed; `hunspell-verdicts.json`, `example-gate.json`, `lemma-extras-gate.json`, `gloss-cache/fr.json`, `synonym-cache/fr.json` regenerated and committed; `packs:check` green (36 files, also in CI fail-closed mode), `bun test` green apart from the pre-existing environmental `readMistralKey` fail.

## Consequences

- `packs:check` fails CI if any of the 36 packs ships a denylisted lemma or a name-listed lemma that is not on `lemma-names-allow.txt`.
- Chip membership now runs for en, de, es, fr, and it as well as pt. `hunspell-verdicts.json` is regenerated when `packs:check` sees new lemmas or chip tokens.
- A replacement lemma has to clear the hint ceiling: its fold must not already be a gloss or synonym token in a lower band, and its own gloss tokens must sit at or below its band. Where the band is already full, or the in-band nouns are those lower-band tokens, the replacement is the next unused noun that clears that ceiling, so it can sit above the band window.
- Revisit if a licensed PT POS source appears, or if #335 changes the `GRÜSSE` lemma.
