## 1. Concept and principles

The concept is **“Cartridge & CRT.”** Hiato is a word game, so it may look like one: chunky 2px outlines, hard drop shadows, notched pixel corners and an arcade stripe. A win throws pixel sparks, and keys press down like cabinet buttons. But Hiato is a learning tool first, so every gloss, hint and accent must read at a glance.

1. **Legibility first.** The pixel font is for short labels, single letters and numbers. Glosses, hints, example sentences, explanations and long copy stay in **Inter**.
2. **Pixel accents, not pixel everything.** Borders, shadows, icons, tiles, keys and badges become pixel art. Layout, spacing, hit areas and reading width stay as they are.
3. **A value swap, not a rebuild. Retro replaces the current look; there is no theme picker.** Every existing token keeps its name and role. Components change through token values and a few CSS utilities. Six tokens are added: `edge`, `shadow`, `focus`, `coin` and `spark`, plus the decorative `scan`.
4. **Colour is never the only cue.** Every state that colour shows also has a shape cue: a pressed key, a dashed outline, a corner mark, or text.
5. **Calm by default.** Motion uses short `steps()` frames and has a reduced-motion version. Sound is off by default and never carries meaning.
6. **Existing copy is untouched.** Every visible string and accessible name in the mocks comes from the current code. The only new strings are the approved About additions: the “Sound effects” switch with its helper line (§5.9) and a Jersey 10 typeface credit (§5.9). See §12. Glossary words are used exactly as written: **Daily, Practice** (never “endless”), **Pocket, Stats, StreakChip, Lives, EndCard, Reveal, Due, Lost, Streak freeze, Offline ready**.

## 2. Palette

Light and dark are both retro. They map 1:1 onto the existing `@theme` names, and they **replace** the current values: the old palette is retired. Shipping means swapping values in `src/index.css` and updating the pinned values in `src/theme.test.ts`.
- **Light, “Cartridge”:** warm cartridge-label paper with a faint 8px dot grid, deep indigo ink, arcade green, and a red/amber/green label stripe.
- **Dark, “CRT night”:** navy-black glass with a 3% scanline overlay, phosphor-cream ink and phosphor-green accents.

### 2.1 Contrast: computed, all pass

The ratios come from the WCAG 2.x relative-luminance formula in `tools/palette.py`. `src/theme.test.ts` uses the same formula with a 0.04045 sRGB threshold instead of 0.03928, which changes nothing here at two decimals.

A second check, `mock/render.js`, walks every text node in the rendered mocks and tests it against its actual opaque background. The bar is 4.5:1, or 3:1 for text that is at least 24px, or at least 18.66px and bold. The result in `mock/audit.json`: `lowContrast: []`, `overflow: []`, `pixelBelowMin18_67px: []`.

- **Text:** all 40 pairs reach at least 4.5:1, so nothing relies on the large-text exception. The lowest is **5.01:1** (dark, white on `accent`).
- **Non-text:** all 44 UI pairs reach at least 3:1; the lowest is **3.44:1** (dark, the `accent` correct-key fill on `raised`). The two `spark` rows are decorative and have no minimum.
- **Fixes I made while iterating:**
  - Dark `accent` moved from `#1c6e42` to `#1f7f4a`. It was 2.75:1 against the pad; white text on it still passes at 5.01.
  - Hard black shadows disappear on CRT navy, so I added the `edge` token. The 2px edge outline now marks every boundary, and the shadow is purely decorative.
  - The light focus ring was 1.22:1 against a green button. It now has two layers: a `cream` gap, then the `focus` ring.
- **Issue #229 is solved inside this theme.** `--color-ink-faint` is set equal to `ink-muted`, so every current `text-ink-faint` use passes AA with no component change. That covers the empty lives, the “·” separators, “Practice isn’t available …”, and the EndCard streak notes. The follow-up is still to remove `ink-faint` from the markup.

## 3. Typography

| Role | Font | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Pixel display: wordmark, headings, tile and key letters, buttons, badges, numbers | **Jersey 10** (SIL OFL 1.1, “Copyright 2023 The Soft Type Project Authors (https://github.com/scfried/soft-type-jersey)”, fetched via `@fontsource/jersey-10`) | Arcade-scoreboard character, and the clearest isolated letters of the six fonts I tested. Isolated letters are what tiles and keys show. |
| Body: glosses, hints, examples, explanations, links, chips, captions | **Inter** 400/500/600 and 400 italic (already shipped, OFL) | Very legible, already precached, and covers every diacritic in the packs |

**How I chose.** I rendered six OFL pixel fonts on the letters learners must tell apart (`assets/font-candidates.png`).

| Font | Verdict | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Pixelify Sans | Rejected | Isolated **E reads as ε**, **2 as Z** and **5 as 8**; my first Stats mock showed “75%” as “78%” |
| Press Start 2P | Rejected | **Ã and Õ read as Ä and Ö**, because the 8px grid squashes the tilde into dots. That is fatal for Portuguese. It is also very wide and has no ẞ. |
| Tiny5 | Rejected | **Ã and Õ read as Ā and Ō**, and **Z looks like 2** |
| Silkscreen | Rejected | Caps only, and no ẞ |
| VT323 | Rejected | Thin terminal strokes that are weak at key size; one weight |
| **Jersey 10** | **Chosen** | E, Z/2, S/5, J, G and Q are distinct. The tilde, circumflex, diaeresis, Ç, ß, ẞ, Œ and Ñ are all clear. It is condensed, so long labels (“Review 1 due pocket word”, “Replace and practice”) fit at 28px. |

**Glyph coverage.** I checked the font’s character map with fontTools (`tools/glyph-coverage.json`). Jersey 10 covers:
- A–Z and a–z
- every key on all six pads in `lib/keyboards.ts` (EN, PT, DE, ES, FR, IT): Á À Â Ã É Ê Í Ó Ô Õ Ú Ç Ü Ö Ä ß Ñ È Ë Î Ï Û Œ Ì Ò Ù, and their lowercase forms
- ẞ, the digits and %, and the punctuation · ’ “ ” — …

The only gap is **←**, used in “← Home” and “← Daily”. Those links are Inter anyway. The font stack is `"Jersey 10", "Inter", system-ui, sans-serif`, so any missing glyph falls back to Inter and never shows as a blank box.

One known quirk: Jersey’s **O and 0** look alike. The pad and tiles show letters only, and the Stats numbers stand alone, so this is acceptable.

**Pixel grid.** Jersey 10 is drawn on a 75/1400 em grid, so one font pixel equals `font-size ÷ 18.667`. Sizes are multiples of **`--fpx: 18.667px`**. For example, 28px is 1.5 fpx, which is exactly 3 device pixels at DPR 2.

Rules:
- **No pixel font below 18.67px (1 fpx).** The mock audit enforces this; anything smaller uses Inter.

- Every pack-language string (gloss, synonyms, word, example) keeps its `lang` attribute.

## 6. Motion and sound

All motion uses `steps(n, end)`: hardware-style frames, not easing curves. The existing duration tokens stay (`--dur-fast` 150ms, `--dur-mid` 220ms, `--dur-slow` 300ms). One is added: `--dur-blink: 480ms`.

| Cue | Today | Retro version | With reduced motion |
|---|---|---|---|
| Letter reveal (`motion-letter-reveal`) | 300ms pop | Tile flip, `scaleY` 1 → 0 → 1 in `steps(4)` over 220ms, then a one-frame 4px pop | Instant; only the colour state changes |
| Wrong key (`motion-key-shake`) | 220ms shake | `translateX` −2 / +2 / −2 / 0px in `steps(4)` over 150ms | No shake; the dashed outline and × still show the miss |
| Key press (`motion-press`) | Scale 0.97 | `translateY(4px)`, drop to 0, one frame | Same (it is a state, not an animation) |
| Life lost (`motion-life-deplete`) | 300ms fade | The seed blinks 3 times (`steps(2)`, 480ms) inside a 2px dashed `danger` outline, then becomes the outline seed | Instant outline seed. The `aria-live` announcement is unchanged. |
| Win (`motion-result-enter` plus the badge pulse) | Fade and slide | The card enters with `translateY(8px → 0)` in `steps(3)` over 220ms. The six sparks run one 4-frame scale-and-opacity loop over 600ms, then hold. | Static sparks, no slide |
| Streak +1 (`motion-streak-pulse`) | 220ms pulse | The flame swaps between 2 frames for 2 loops, then the count ticks | The count updates with no animation |
| Screen change (“insert coin”) | — | Optional 150ms `steps(3)` left-to-right wipe of the new page’s top stripe. Content never animates. | Off |
| Sheets | None | None (keeps the #85 / #221 rule) | None |

- Nothing loops forever, and nothing flashes more than three times a second (WCAG 2.3.1). The life blink is a small area and runs three cycles only.
- The ADR 0041 Playwright baselines already force `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`, so snapshots stay deterministic.

**Sound cues (in scope, decision 3)**

Rules:
- **Off by default.** Sound plays only after the user turns on the About “Sound effects” switch (§5.9).
- **No autoplay.** A cue only plays as the direct result of a user tap or key press. That includes the tap that ends a round, so the win and loss cues qualify. Nothing plays on page load, on a timer or on a route change, and nothing plays while `document.hidden`.
- **The audio context starts on a gesture.** One shared `AudioContext` is created lazily on the first tap with sound on (the switch tap itself counts), and resumed on later taps if the browser suspended it.
- **Never the only carrier of meaning.** Every cue repeats a state that is already on screen and announced: a key state, the `N lives remaining` live region, the EndCard badge, or the StreakChip. With sound off, nothing is lost.
- **Respects the device’s silent setting where possible.**
  - Where supported, set `navigator.audioSession.type = 'ambient'` (Safari 16.4+; feature-detected). On iOS, the Ring/Silent switch then mutes the cues, and they mix with the user’s music instead of stopping it.
  - Never use `'playback'` or the silent-`<audio>` trick, which override the switch.
  - Other platforms follow the system media volume.
  - The EndCard mute note stays as it is.
- **Polite to speech.** Cues are skipped while `speechSynthesis.speaking`, so they never talk over the EndCard word.
- **Quiet and short.** A master gain of 0.08 with a 5ms attack/release, so there are no clicks. Every cue is **≤ 600ms**. A new cue cuts the previous one, so they never pile up.
- **Works offline.** Everything is synthesised with Web Audio `OscillatorNode` square and triangle waves plus a `GainNode` envelope. **No audio files and no new dependencies**, about 60 lines of code.
  - Tiny precached files are not justified: oscillators already give the chiptune sound, and they add zero bytes to the precache and nothing that can fail offline.
- **Reduced motion** does not change sound. The switch is the only control.

| Cue | Fires when | Sound | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|
| `key-correct` | A letter tap reveals at least one tile | Square wave, C6 → E6 | 2 × 50ms (≈100ms) |
| `key-miss` | A letter tap is a miss (the key becomes dashed, and lives drop by 1) | Triangle wave, A3 with a 60-cent drop | 120ms |
| `life-lost` | A miss leaves 1 life (last-life warning), in sync with the seed blink | 3 falling square ticks, E5 / C5 / A4 | 3 × 60ms (≈200ms) |
| `hint` | The “Hint — reveal one accent” tap reveals an accent | Soft triangle ping, G5 | 140ms |
| `win` | A round ends in a win: Daily, Practice or Pocket (the EndCard “You got it”) | “Stage clear” arpeggio, C5–E5–G5–C6–E6 | 5 × 90ms (≈500ms) |
| `loss` | A round ends “Out of lives”, or with a Practice “Word revealed” | 3 gentle falling triangle notes, G4 / E4 / C4 | 3 × 140ms (≈450ms) |
| `streak` | A Daily win raises the streak (with the StreakChip pulse), played 300ms after `win` | Coin “bling”, B5 → E6 | 2 × 60ms (≈120ms) |
| `freeze` | A Daily win earns a Streak freeze (with the ❄️ → freeze icon change) | Two high sine blips, A6 | 2 × 50ms |
| `coin` | The About switch is turned **on** | Coin “bling”, B5 → E6 | ≈120ms |

Nothing plays when sheets open or close, on Share, on Remove or Retry, on navigation, or when the switch is turned off.

## 7. Accessibility

- **Contrast** (§2.1):
  - All text is at least 4.5:1, and all UI boundaries are at least 3:1, in both themes.
  - `ink-faint` is retired by aliasing it to `ink-muted` (#229).
- **Touch targets:**
  - Every control stays at least 44×44: 48px key hit boxes, `min-h-11` buttons, and 44px Close and speaker buttons.
  - The drop shadow sits outside the hit box, and pressing moves only the painted face.
- **Readable type:**
  - No pixel font below 18.67px, and none for sentences.
  - Glosses, hints and examples are Inter at 14px or more; 12px is for captions only, as today.
  - No all-caps beyond single letters and “Accents”.
  - Body line height is about 1.45.
- **Legibility and dyslexia:**
  - Jersey 10 was chosen for distinct isolated letters (§3).
  - The EndCard word is real text with a `lang` attribute, and Inter has no added tracking.
  - The dot grid was checked at its worst case: `accent-fg` on a `cream-dark` dot is 5.33:1 in light mode.
  - `prefers-contrast: more` removes the dot grid, scanlines, stripe and sparks.
- **Colour is never alone:**
  - Correct keys are pressed with a ✓.
  - Misses are pressed and dashed with a ×.
  - Vowel-help tiles are dashed and captioned.
  - Lives are filled or outlined.
  - Due and Lost have text badges.
- **Screen-reader names are unchanged:**
  - `Letter pad`, `Accent keys`, `Word letters`
  - `N lives remaining`, `Streak N, k freeze(s)`
  - `Pocket, n words, k due for review`, `Stats, n rounds`
  - `Win rate, n percent`, `Average lives left on wins, …`, `k lives left: n wins`, `Lost: n rounds`
  - `Reveal word and end this practice round`, and `speakLemmaAriaLabel` (for example “Ouvir a palavra”)
  - New: the About switch is `role="switch"` with `aria-checked`, named “Sound effects” and described by its helper line.
  - All new icons, sparks and share-card tiles are `aria-hidden`.
- **Focus:**
  - Always visible on `:focus-visible`: a 3px amber `focus` ring outside a 3px `cream` gap.
  - It reaches at least 3:1 against the page, cards and hover fills, and the gap separates it from green buttons.
  - Focus order and dialog focus rules (#84, #85, #221) are unchanged.
- **Reduced motion:** see §6.
- **Zoom:** layouts reflow at 200% text zoom, and type uses CSS sizes, never text baked into images.

This is for later, once a ticket exists. Nothing here is for Buster now.

1. **Replace the look (decision 1): a token-value swap, not a theme.**
